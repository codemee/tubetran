## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **修正：在投影片模式中切到其他影片時，現在會自動退出投影片模式。** 以前會停留在投影片模式，新影片也沒有 YouTube 的控制列、而且靜音；現在新影片會用正常的播放器開啟。刪除或重新處理目前這支影片時也一樣。

- **Fixed: switching to another video while in slide mode now leaves slide mode.** Previously slide mode stayed on, and the new video had no YouTube controls and was muted; now it opens in the normal player. The same applies when you delete or reprocess the current video.
