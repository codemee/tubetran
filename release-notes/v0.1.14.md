## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **改善：投影片模式的畫面不再有 YouTube 的播放鈕、標題列。** 第一次進入某支影片的投影片模式時，會在背景下載影片（只要影像，1080p 優先），在每則字幕結束前擷取一張圖，再刪掉影片；之後切換是即時的。右上角顯示準備進度（10 分鐘的影片約 30～40 秒、圖片約 20 MB），已經好的畫面先顯示。
- **新增：刪除投影片。** 有擷取好的畫面時，工具列會出現垃圾桶圖示，可刪除畫面省空間（會顯示佔用多少空間）；下次進入投影片模式會重新擷取。
- **改善：按鈕改成圖示。** 播放器下方的工具列（投影片模式、刪除投影片、下載 SRT／VTT、匯出）與影片庫上方的匯入、匯出全部都改成圖示，名稱在滑鼠移上去時顯示。
- **修正：** 「下載 SRT／VTT」與「匯出」在翻譯完成前就出現；工具列寬度剛好時「匯出」被擠到下一行；投影片畫面兩側有細黑線。

- **Improved: slide mode no longer shows YouTube's play button and title bar.** The first time you enter slide mode for a video, TubeTran downloads the video in the background (picture only, 1080p preferred), captures one image just before each subtitle ends, then deletes the video; switching is instant from then on. Progress is shown at the top right (about 30–40 seconds and 20 MB of images for a 10-minute video), and images are shown as soon as they are ready.
- **New: delete slides.** When a video has captured images, a trash-can icon appears in the toolbar to delete them and save space (it shows how much space they use); slide mode captures them again next time.
- **Improved: icon buttons.** The toolbar below the player (slide mode, delete slides, download SRT / VTT, export) and Import / Export all at the top of the library are now icons; hover to see their names.
- **Fixed:** "Download SRT / VTT" and "Export" appeared before translation finished; "Export" wrapped to a new line when the toolbar was just wide enough; thin black lines on both sides of slide images.
