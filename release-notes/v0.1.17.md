## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：搜尋字幕。** 工具列「刪除投影片」旁邊多了放大鏡，按下去展開搜尋列：
  - 可選「不分大小寫」「完整單字」「規則表達式」；命中的地方在字幕清單中反白，目前這個顏色較深、有外框。
  - ⌃ ⌄ 按鈕（或 Shift+Enter／Enter）跳到上一個／下一個命中，清單捲到那一行；顯示「第幾個／共幾個」。
  - 勾選「影片也跳過去」時，影片也會跳到命中的那一則（投影片模式則換到那一張）。
  - 選項會記住；按 Esc 或再按一次放大鏡關閉。

- **New: search subtitles.** A magnifier next to "Delete slides" in the toolbar opens a search bar:
  - Options for ignore case, whole word and regular expression; matches are highlighted in the subtitle list, the current one darker with an outline.
  - ⌃ ⌄ buttons (or Shift+Enter / Enter) go to the previous / next match and scroll the list to that line; "match / total" is shown.
  - With "Jump the video too" on, the video also jumps to the matching line (in slide mode, to that slide).
  - The options are remembered; press Esc or the magnifier again to close.
