## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **改善：網址欄。** 點一下網址欄會全選文字，方便直接貼上新網址；按 Enter 或「載入」後，鍵盤焦點離開輸入欄，Space、方向鍵、M 等播放器快捷鍵馬上有效，不會再把字打進網址欄。
- **修正：** 影片還在翻譯時直接移除，右側進度卡不再一直停在「cancelling」；進度卡的「取消中」也補上中英文標籤。

- **Improved: URL box.** Clicking the box selects all its text, so you can paste a new URL right away; after Enter or "Load", keyboard focus leaves the box and player shortcuts such as Space, arrow keys and M work immediately, with no stray typing into the box.
- **Fixed:** removing a video while it is still being translated no longer leaves the progress card stuck on "cancelling"; the cancelling state also has a proper Chinese/English label now.
