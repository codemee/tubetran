## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：顯示版本編號。** 設定面板底部會顯示 `TubeTran v0.1.9`（點一下可前往發行版本）；命令列也可用 `tubetran --version`。
- **新增：回到 YouTube 的連結。** 影片清單的每個項目、以及播放器標題下方都有「在 YouTube 開啟」，方便查看留言與相關影片；桌面版會用系統預設瀏覽器開啟。
- **新增：處理中的影片也能取消並刪除。** 以前處理中沒有刪除鈕；現在按 ✕ 會取消背景任務，任務在目前這一步結束後自行清除快取（已呼叫 Claude 的費用無法退回）。

- **New: version number.** The settings panel now shows `TubeTran v0.1.9` at the bottom (click it to open the releases page); on the command line, `tubetran --version` prints it too.
- **New: link back to YouTube.** Every library item and the title under the player have an "Open on YouTube" link for comments and related videos; the desktop app opens it in your default browser.
- **New: cancel and delete a video that is still processing.** Previously there was no delete button while a video was processing; now ✕ cancels the background job, which cleans up its cache after the current step (Claude calls already made are not refunded).
