## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：字幕高低位置可以調整。** 字幕擋到 YouTube 進度條時，播放中按 **Alt+↑** 把字幕往上、**Alt+↓** 往下，按住不放會連續移動：
  - 視窗與全螢幕**各記一個位置**（兩種畫面的進度條高度不一樣），記在這台電腦上，下次開啟沿用；沒調整過就維持原本的預設位置。
  - 用 Alt 組合鍵是為了不和 YouTube 的音量快捷鍵（單純的 ↑ ↓）衝突。
  - 桌面版（Windows）點過影片之後也能用；瀏覽器模式要先點一下影片外面。

- **New: adjustable subtitle height.** When the subtitles cover YouTube's progress bar, press **Alt+↑** during playback to move them up and **Alt+↓** to move them down; holding the keys keeps moving:
  - Windowed and fullscreen **each remember their own position** (the progress bar sits at a different height in the two views), saved on this computer and kept next time; until you adjust it, the default position is used.
  - Alt is used so the keys don't clash with YouTube's volume shortcut (plain ↑ ↓).
  - In the desktop app (Windows) it also works after you have clicked the video; in browser mode, click somewhere outside the video first.
