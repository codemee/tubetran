## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **全螢幕鈕移到播放器右下角，並跟著 YouTube 控制列一起顯示／隱藏**（位置在 YouTube 字樣旁）。桌面版會偵測控制列是否顯示；瀏覽器無法偵測，所以滑鼠在播放區時一直顯示。
- **新增 `F` 快捷鍵切換全螢幕。** 桌面版在點過影片之後也有效（點過影片後鍵盤焦點在 YouTube 的 iframe 裡，網頁本來收不到按鍵）。
- **字幕位置下移。**

- **The fullscreen button moved to the player's bottom right and now shows and hides together with YouTube's own controls** (next to the YouTube wordmark). The desktop app detects whether the control bar is visible; a browser cannot, so there the button stays visible while the pointer is over the player.
- **New `F` shortcut for fullscreen.** In the desktop app it also works after you have clicked the video (after a click, keyboard focus is inside YouTube's iframe, where the page normally can't receive keys).
- **Subtitles sit lower.**
