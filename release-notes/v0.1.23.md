## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：重新開啟程式時，自動載入上次看的影片。** 影片會停在上次的播放位置，不會自動播放，按播放就能接著看：
  - 只有「已處理完成、還在影片庫裡」的影片才會自動載入；處理中、失敗或已刪除的不會，避免你沒操作就開始處理、產生翻譯費用。
  - 設定面板多了「啟動時載入上次觀看的影片」選項，預設開啟；只記在這台電腦上，切換後立即生效，不需按「儲存」。

- **New: reopening the app loads the video you watched last.** It is parked at the last playback position without autoplay, so you just press play to continue:
  - Only videos that are finished and still in the library are loaded this way; in-progress, failed or deleted ones are not, so nothing starts processing (and costing translation fees) without you doing anything.
  - The settings panel has a new "Load the last video on startup" option, on by default; it is saved on this computer only and takes effect right away, without pressing Save.
