## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **字幕位置自動跟著 YouTube 控制列走。** 控制列（進度條）出現時，字幕貼在進度條上方；控制列收起時，字幕落到播放器下緣，不用再手動調。
  - 原本的 Alt+↑／↓ 手動調整改成**選項**，預設關閉：工具列「雙語／原文／譯文／無」右邊新增「字幕位置」鈕（兩條橫線加上下箭頭），按下開啟後才套用手動位置、Alt+↑／↓ 才有作用；全螢幕時最上排也有同一顆（同一個開關）。
  - 以前用 Alt+↑／↓ 記下的位置還在，開啟選項就會套用。

- **修正全螢幕鈕有時不見、按了沒用。** 新版 YouTube 在右下角多了一顆「YouTube」膠囊，滑鼠一碰就蓋住全螢幕鈕。現在游標移到膠囊最右端時按鈕一定會出現，而且膠囊本身（點了到 YouTube 觀看）維持原本的作用。
  - 全螢幕鈕的圖示改成左上、右下兩個對角箭頭，位置也微調過。

- **術語表更順手，也更不容易弄丟修改。**
  - 術語表鈕移到「字幕清單」那一列的右端，面板展開在字幕清單下方；和字幕清單互斥，展開其中一個會自動收起另一個。
  - 有尚未儲存的修改時，字典圖示右上角會出現小圓點，面板也會提示。這時切換影片，或用任何方式關閉程式（右上角 ×、Alt+F4、工作列關閉視窗），會先跳出確認。

- **所有確認視窗統一成跟應用程式一致的樣式。** 刪除影片、重新處理、重新翻譯、重設術語表、刪除投影片、放棄未儲存的修改，都改用自製的對話框（跟著淺色／深色主題），按鈕文字也配合動作（例如「刪除」「重新翻譯」「繼續編輯」）。

- **The subtitle position now follows YouTube's controls automatically.** While the controls (progress bar) are showing, the subtitles sit just above the progress bar; when the controls hide, they drop to the bottom edge of the player, so there is no need to adjust them by hand.
  - The manual Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ adjustment is now an **option**, off by default: a new "Subtitle position" button (two horizontal lines with up/down arrows) sits right of the "Bilingual / Original / Translation / None" options in the toolbar. Turn it on and your manual position applies and Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ work; in fullscreen the same button is in the top row (one switch).
  - Positions you saved earlier with Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ are kept and apply as soon as you turn the option on.

- **Fixed: the fullscreen button sometimes disappearing or not responding.** New YouTube players show a "YouTube" capsule at the bottom right that covered our button when the pointer touched it. Now the button always appears when the pointer reaches the right end of the capsule, and the capsule itself (which opens the video on YouTube) keeps working as before.
  - The fullscreen icon is now two diagonal arrows (top-left and bottom-right), and its position was fine-tuned.

- **A smoother glossary, and fewer lost edits.**
  - The glossary button moved to the right end of the transcript row, and its panel opens below the transcript; the two are mutually exclusive, so opening one collapses the other.
  - With unsaved changes, the dictionary icon shows a small dot and the panel says so. If you then switch videos or close the app in any way (the × at the top right, Alt+F4, closing the window from the taskbar), you are asked first.

- **All confirmation windows now match the app.** Deleting a video, reprocessing, retranslating, resetting the glossary, deleting slide images and discarding unsaved edits all use a custom dialog (following the light / dark theme), with button labels that match the action (such as "Delete", "Retranslate", "Keep editing").
