## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：字幕書籤。** 替重要的字幕做記號，之後一鍵跳回去：
  - 滑鼠移到字幕清單的某一行，左側浮現書籤圖示；按下後輸入名稱（Enter 儲存、Esc 取消）就加入書籤。已有書籤的行固定顯示實心圖示與名稱，可再改名或刪除。
  - 工具列搜尋鈕旁多了書籤鈕（這支影片有書籤時才出現）：按下列出全部書籤，點一個就讓影片跳到對應的字幕（投影片模式則換到那一張），清單也捲到那一行；滑過項目會浮現 ✕，可直接刪除書籤。
  - 書籤跟著影片存在本機，匯出／匯入影片時一起帶走；重新處理影片後，書籤會對到時間最接近的那一句。

- **New: subtitle bookmarks.** Mark important lines and jump back to them with one click:
  - Hover a line in the subtitle list and a bookmark icon appears at its left; press it, type a name (Enter to save, Esc to cancel) and the bookmark is added. A bookmarked line keeps a filled icon and its name, and can be renamed or deleted.
  - A bookmark button next to the magnifier in the toolbar (shown only when the video has bookmarks) lists them all; click one to jump the video to that line (in slide mode, to that slide) with the list scrolled to it; hover an item and a ✕ appears to delete the bookmark.
  - Bookmarks are saved locally with the video and travel with it when you export / import; after reprocessing a video, a bookmark lands on the line closest in time.
