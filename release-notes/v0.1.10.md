## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：標籤篩選的「無標籤」。** 標籤列最前面固定有「無標籤」（後面的數字是還沒加標籤的影片數），點了只列出沒有任何標籤的影片，方便找出還沒整理的影片。它和一般標籤互斥；沒有符合的影片時，清單就是空的。
- **改善：標籤很多時不再把清單往下擠。** 標籤列平時只顯示一行，放不下的收進最後面的「更多 +N」，點了展開全部（最高約 4 行，再多可捲動），再按「收合」。「清除篩選」固定在標籤列右側，不會被捲到看不見。
- **改善：網址輸入欄位的右緣現在和下方播放器的右緣對齊**（視窗縮放、桌面版無框視窗、最大化時都會跟著對齊）。

- **New: "Untagged" in the tag filter.** The tag bar now always starts with "Untagged" (the number is how many videos have no tags yet); click it to list only the videos without any tags, which makes it easy to find the ones you have not organised yet. It is mutually exclusive with the regular tags, and when nothing matches the list is simply empty.
- **Improved: many tags no longer push the library down.** The tag bar normally shows a single row; tags that do not fit are folded into "More +N" at the end, which expands them all (up to about 4 rows, then it scrolls) and turns into "Less". "Clear filters" is fixed on the right of the bar so it never scrolls out of view.
- **Improved: the right edge of the URL field now lines up with the right edge of the player below** (also when resizing, in the frameless desktop window and when maximized).
