## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **修正：按全螢幕鈕後，焦點會回到播放器。** 以前焦點停在全螢幕鈕上，← → 無法倒轉、快轉；現在進入全螢幕就能直接用（投影片模式則是上一則、下一則）。
- **改善：翻譯失敗時看得到原因，也能直接重試。** 錯誤訊息會附上最後一次失敗的原因（例如「回覆超過上限被截斷」「回覆少了幾句」）；狀態列下方有「重試」鈕。影片庫的卡片會標成「翻譯失敗」並有重試鈕，滑鼠移上去可看到原因。重試從中斷處接著翻，已翻好的句子不會重翻。
- **修正：降低翻譯失敗的機會。** 每批的輸出上限從 8192 提高到 16000 個 token；模型會先花一部分 token 思考，原本的上限偶爾不夠，譯文被截斷就會三次都失敗。

- **Fixed: focus returns to the player after pressing the fullscreen button.** Previously focus stayed on the button, so ← → didn't seek; now they work right away in fullscreen (in slide mode they go to the previous / next line).
- **Improved: failed translations show the reason and can be retried.** The error now includes why the last attempt failed (for example "the reply was cut off at the limit" or "the reply was missing lines"), with a "Retry" button below the status line. The library card is marked "Translation failed" with a retry button, and hovering shows the reason. Retrying continues where it stopped; lines already translated are kept.
- **Fixed: translation fails less often.** The per-batch output limit was raised from 8192 to 16000 tokens; the model spends part of it thinking first, and the old limit was occasionally too small, cutting off the reply so all three attempts failed.
