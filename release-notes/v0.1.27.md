## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：術語表可以手動編輯。** 影片翻完後，播放器下方工具列多了一顆字典圖示：按下去可以檢視這支影片的術語表、修改譯法、新增或刪除術語（譯法寫成原文就是保留不翻）。
  - 「儲存」只存起來；「儲存並重新翻譯」會用修改後的術語表把整支影片的字幕與標題重翻一遍（原文字幕沿用、不重抓；標籤、書籤、播放位置、投影片都保留）。重翻會呼叫 Claude，按下前會先確認。
  - 手動編輯過的術語表，之後在影片庫按 ↻ 重新處理時會保留；想要全新的自動整理，按面板裡的「重設為自動整理」。
  - 術語多的時候，面板上方有搜尋框，可同時搜原文與譯法。
  - 匯出／匯入影片時，「手動編輯過」的標記也會一起帶走。

- **New: edit the glossary by hand.** Once a video is translated, a dictionary icon appears in the toolbar under the player. Open it to view this video's glossary, change translations, and add or remove terms (use the original word as the translation to keep it untranslated).
  - "Save" only stores your changes; "Save and retranslate" translates the whole video's subtitles and title again with the edited glossary (the original captions are reused, not downloaded again; tags, bookmarks, playback position and slides are kept). Retranslating calls Claude, so you are asked to confirm first.
  - A glossary you have edited is kept when you later reprocess the video with ↻ in the library; press "Reset to automatic" in the panel if you want a fresh automatic one.
  - A search box at the top of the panel finds terms by original or translation when the list is long.
  - The "edited by hand" mark travels with the video when you export / import it.
