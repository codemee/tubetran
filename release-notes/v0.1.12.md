## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **改善：無字幕影片會顯示語音辨識的每個階段。** 以前只有一句「正在辨識語音」，等很久也不知道在做什麼。現在進度條下方會列出：下載音檔、下載語音辨識模型（只有第一次）、載入模型、解碼音檔與偵測語言、辨識語音；完成的打勾，進行中的標示出來，下載音檔、下載模型與辨識語音都有百分比。

- **Improved: videos without captions now show each stage of speech recognition.** Previously there was only "Transcribing speech…", with no hint of what was taking so long. The progress card now lists: download audio, download the speech model (first time only), load the model, decode audio and detect language, transcribe; completed stages are ticked, the current one is highlighted, and downloading the audio, downloading the model and transcribing show a percentage.
