## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **新增：全螢幕時可以隨時切換字幕模式。** 播放中按 **Alt+C**，依序切換 **雙語 → 原文 → 譯文 → 無**：
  - 全螢幕時播放器最上排（YouTube 音量圖示的左邊）多了一顆字幕模式鈕，點一下同樣依序切換，會跟全螢幕鈕一起顯示與隱藏。
  - 切換時畫面上方中央會短暫顯示目前模式；選擇的模式記在這台電腦上，下次開啟沿用，工具列的選項也會同步。
  - 用 Alt 組合鍵是為了不和 YouTube 自己的 `C`（開關字幕）衝突，YouTube 原生的控制照常可用。
  - 桌面版（Windows）點過影片之後也能用；瀏覽器模式要先點一下影片外面。

- **新增：全螢幕時可以帶出書籤清單。** 這支影片有書籤時，按 **Alt+B**（或最上排的書籤鈕）在播放區裡列出書籤：
  - 用 **↑ ↓** 選、**Enter** 跳到對應的字幕、再按一次 **Alt+B** 關閉，也可以直接用滑鼠點；開啟時從目前播放位置之前最近的書籤開始選。
  - 開啟時鍵盤焦點交給清單，所以 ↑ ↓ 不會動到 YouTube 的音量；關閉或跳轉後焦點還給播放器。
  - 全螢幕的清單只能選取跳轉，不提供刪除，避免誤刪；要刪除請回到視窗模式。
  - 視窗模式按 Alt+B 則是開關工具列的書籤清單。

- **New: switch the subtitle mode any time in fullscreen.** Press **Alt+C** during playback to cycle **Bilingual → Original → Translation → None**:
  - In fullscreen there is a subtitle-mode button in the top row of the player (left of YouTube's volume icon); click it to cycle the same way. It shows and hides together with the fullscreen button.
  - The current mode flashes briefly at the top center; the chosen mode is saved on this computer and kept next time, and the toolbar options stay in sync.
  - Alt is used so it doesn't clash with YouTube's own `C` caption toggle, which keeps working as usual.
  - In the desktop app (Windows) it also works after you have clicked the video; in browser mode, click somewhere outside the video first.

- **New: bring up the bookmark list in fullscreen.** When the video has bookmarks, press **Alt+B** (or the bookmark button in the top row) to list them over the player:
  - Use **↑ ↓** to choose, **Enter** to jump to that subtitle, press **Alt+B** again to close; the mouse works too. It starts on the nearest bookmark before the current playback position.
  - Keyboard focus moves to the list while it is open, so ↑ ↓ don't change YouTube's volume; closing or jumping gives focus back to the player.
  - The fullscreen list only jumps; it has no delete button, to avoid accidental deletes. Delete bookmarks in windowed mode.
  - In windowed mode, Alt+B opens and closes the toolbar's bookmark list.

- **修正：全螢幕時 Space／K 無法播放暫停。** 用 `F` 進入全螢幕、或進入前焦點不在影片上時，YouTube 的快捷鍵收不到按鍵（YouTube 需要焦點在播放器內部的元素上，程式無法替它設定）。現在全螢幕時由 TubeTran 自己處理同樣的快捷鍵：`Space`／`K` 播放暫停、`←` `→` 5 秒、`J` `L` 10 秒、`M` 靜音、`↑` `↓` 音量；點過影片後焦點在 YouTube 裡，則仍由 YouTube 原生處理。

- **Fix: Space / K didn't play or pause in fullscreen.** When you entered fullscreen with `F`, or the focus wasn't on the video beforehand, YouTube never received the keys (it needs focus on an element inside the player, which a page can't set for it). In fullscreen TubeTran now handles the same shortcuts itself: `Space` / `K` play and pause, `←` `→` seek 5 s, `J` `L` 10 s, `M` mutes, `↑` `↓` change the volume; after you click the video, focus is inside YouTube and its own handling applies as before.
