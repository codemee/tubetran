## 本版重點 / Highlights

- **修正：繁體中文字幕不再送去翻譯。** YouTube 常把繁體中文字幕只標成「中文」（`zh`），過去因此整支影片仍會跑術語表與翻譯，浪費時間與費用。現在會看字幕內容判斷繁簡：
  - 是繁體中文：不呼叫 Claude API，原文直接當作譯文；雙語模式、搜尋與 SRT／VTT 也只顯示一次，不重複同一句。
  - 是簡體中文：照常翻成繁體中文（台灣用語）。
  - 已經翻過的影片，到影片庫按 ↻ 重新處理即可套用。

- **Fixed: Traditional Chinese captions are no longer translated.** YouTube often labels Traditional Chinese captions as plain "Chinese" (`zh`), so such videos still went through the glossary and translation, wasting time and cost. The app now checks the caption text to tell Traditional from Simplified:
  - Traditional Chinese: the Claude API is not called and the original is used as the translation; bilingual mode, search and SRT / VTT show each line once.
  - Simplified Chinese: translated into Traditional Chinese (Taiwan usage) as before.
  - For videos translated earlier, press ↻ in the library to reprocess and apply this.
